The Research and Production Center LLC "KERAMIKA SINTEZ" has completed a major modernization of its official web portal.

## Goals of Modernization

The update systematically organizes extensive research materials, field test reports, technical calculations, and methodological guides developed under the scientific supervision of Professor Rustam Kh. Rakhimov.

The portal highlights core engineering areas:
* Industrial and domestic drying installations for agricultural products;
* Cascade polymer solar radiation converters for greenhouses;
* High-efficiency pulsed infrared sterilization equipment;
* Energy-efficient baking conveyors and roasting ovens;
* Equipment for raw cotton drying and seed germination stimulation;
* Curing systems for industrial paint and protective coatings;
* Therapeutic pulsed infrared emitters and medical lamps.

The materials are provided in both Russian and English to facilitate international scientific and technical cooperation.
