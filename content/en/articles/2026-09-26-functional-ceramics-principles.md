Functional ceramics represent complex composite materials based on oxides of rare-earth and transition elements synthesized using high-temperature melting techniques at the Big Solar Furnace (BSF).

## Mechanism of Selective Radiation

Unlike conventional ohmic heating elements that radiate across the continuous blackbody spectrum, functional ceramics generate narrowband pulsed infrared radiation within strictly defined wavelength intervals.

Key advantages of the method:
* Direct resonance between radiation quanta and vibrational modes of water and organic molecular bonds;
* Prevention of thermal degradation in surface layers of the processed material;
* Significantly higher energy efficiency compared to standard electric heaters (energy savings of 40–70%);
* Comprehensive sterilization at low processing temperatures (from 40 °C).

The utilization of these materials in the "Feruza" and "Uzbekistan" drying installations confirms complete retention of biologically active compounds, vitamins, and organoleptic properties without chemical additives.
